# Decision Log

## D-001 — Keep SEM Asset Review Separate

Status: Accepted

Decision:
The SEM internal asset-review opportunity is separate from the
YouTube Intelligence MVP.

Reason:
It represents a different business problem and expanding into it
would compromise the current investigation.

Source:
Meeting with Danilo.

---

## D-002 — Prediction Is Not the Initial Objective

Status: Accepted

Decision:
The MVP will investigate relationships between content and
performance before attempting predictive modelling.

Reason:
The underlying relationship has not yet been established.

---

## D-003 — Do Not Assume Individual Creators Are the Customer

Status: Accepted

Decision:
Commercial users remain an open question.

Potential users include agencies, media companies, creator networks
and brand/marketing organisations.

Reason:
Danilo challenged the assumption that individual African creators
would pay for dedicated software.

---

## D-004 — Do Not Build Before Understanding Data Constraints

Status: Accepted

Decision:
Architecture and implementation will follow data/API feasibility
research.

Reason:
The availability and resolution of performance data may determine
whether the proposed investigation is technically possible.
