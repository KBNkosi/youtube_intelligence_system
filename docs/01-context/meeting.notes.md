# SEM / Danilo Meeting Notes

## Date

18 August 2026

## Participants

- Kenny Nkosi
- Danilo Acquisto

## Purpose

Discuss the YouTube Intelligence System and potential SEM collaboration.

## Business Problems Discussed

- SEM currently analyses YouTube performance and provides guidance to brands and creators.
- Some performance analysis currently involves interpretation or conjecture around why content performs the way it does.
- SEM wants to better understand what contributes to YouTube content performance.
- Areas discussed included retention, watch time, views, SEO, thumbnails, audience clarity, hooks, CTAs, video length and other content characteristics.
- There is interest in identifying where improvements could have the greatest impact rather than relying only on individual video performance.
- Danilo described the possibility of using technology to analyse channel-level data and provide more useful performance insights.

## Danilo's Product Vision

- A system that can analyse YouTube channel and video performance.
- The system could examine performance data alongside the content itself.
- It could help identify what is working and what is not working.
- It could identify potential reasons for underperformance.
- It could provide recommendations for future content.
- Potential recommendations discussed included changes to video length, hooks, CTAs and other content characteristics.
- The system could potentially provide recurring insights after content is published.
- A future version could analyse multiple creators or channels through a central content-owner account.
- Danilo described the possibility of using the system to help creators improve future production cycles rather than simply explaining historical performance.

## Technical Questions Raised

- How will the system obtain YouTube data?
- Will data be accessed through APIs or manually uploaded?
- Can the system connect directly to YouTube channels or an existing content-owner account?
- What information will be required from SEM?
- What models and technologies will be required to analyse video, audio and transcript information?
- How will visual elements such as body language be analysed?
- What server/infrastructure requirements will the system have?
- How will multiple channels potentially be handled through a central backend?
- How could the system integrate with existing systems used by SEM?
- Whether the available YouTube data can support the type of analysis being proposed was identified as something that needs to be investigated.

## Commercial Concerns

- Danilo questioned who would ultimately pay for the system.
- The initial idea of targeting individual creators was challenged because many individual African creators may not be able to afford software licensing.
- A B2B market was discussed as a potentially more viable direction.
- Danilo questioned whether building a new system would make sense given that existing international software products are already available.
- The system would therefore need to demonstrate sufficient value or differentiation to justify building or using it.
- Danilo indicated that SEM is willing to explore the project but does not yet know Kenny's capabilities.
- Danilo stated that if the required capability cannot be demonstrated, SEM could use professional software developers or existing software instead.

## Potential SEM Data / Access

- SEM has access to multiple YouTube channels through an Affiliate Content Owner arrangement.
- Danilo indicated that this provides a potential route to API-based access to channel and content data.
- Multiple channels can potentially be managed through the same backend.
- SEM has internal YouTube performance data and analysts.
- SEM may be able to provide information and access required for testing, subject to the appropriate requirements being identified.
- The exact data and API access available still needs to be established.

## Potential Pilot

- Danilo identified the Jungle Collective as a potential testing environment.
- The Jungle Collective consists of approximately 45 creators.
- A future system could potentially analyse performance across multiple creators and provide recurring insights.
- The pilot environment was discussed as a potential way to test the system with real creator/channel data.

## Separate SEM Opportunity

- Danilo described a separate internal SEM problem involving the manual review of content assets.
- SEM currently reviews assets such as videos, graphics and carousels against briefs, brand guidelines and defined criteria.
- This review process requires people to manually assess submitted assets.
- Danilo discussed the possibility of an AI system performing a first-pass review against defined criteria.
- The system could potentially reduce the amount of manual review required.
- This was discussed as a separate potential co-build/co-investment opportunity.
- Danilo indicated that the YouTube Intelligence System should remain Kenny's own project, with SEM potentially acting as a test subject.
- The two systems could potentially share technologies such as visual recognition and transcript analysis in the future.
- No decision was made to merge the two systems at this stage.

## Directly Relevant Statements

- Danilo stated that existing YouTube performance analysis can involve conjecture around why content performs the way it does.
- Danilo questioned why the proposed system could not connect directly to YouTube through an API rather than relying on manual uploads.
- Danilo described the potential use of SEM's Affiliate Content Owner access for managing multiple YouTube channels.
- Danilo identified the Jungle Collective as a potential pilot group.
- Danilo questioned the commercial viability of targeting individual African creators.
- Danilo questioned why SEM or others would build the system rather than license an existing product.
- Danilo raised the possibility that an African-focused system could have value where generic international tools lack local nuance.
- Danilo described the internal asset-review problem as another potential technology opportunity.
- Danilo proposed that the YouTube Intelligence System remain Kenny's project while SEM could provide testing and feedback.
- Danilo requested that Kenny develop the technical architecture, identify the required tools, models, technologies and information, and communicate the requirements to SEM.
- Danilo encouraged Kenny to take the necessary time to produce quality work rather than rushing the architecture.

## Implications for Project

- The YouTube Intelligence System has moved from a theoretical investigation to a project with a potential real-world testing environment.
- SEM is potentially able to provide access to real channel and performance data.
- The architecture needs to account for API-based data ingestion rather than assuming manual uploads.
- The availability and limitations of YouTube data need to be established before finalising the technical design.
- The relationship between retention data and actual video/transcript content remains a central technical question.
- The broader product vision extends beyond identifying individual retention drop-offs toward channel-level performance analysis and actionable recommendations.
- Commercial viability and differentiation remain unresolved.
- The YouTube Intelligence System and SEM's internal content-review system should remain separate at the MVP stage.
- The immediate deliverable from Kenny is the technical architecture and a clear definition of the information/data required from SEM.
